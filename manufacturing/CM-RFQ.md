# Contract Manufacturer RFQ Package

Request quote for EVT 20, DVT 50, PVT 200, and production 1k/5k.

CM scope desired:
- sheet metal or enclosure subassembly.
- PCB assembly and programming.
- harness manufacture.
- printer/display/lock integration.
- full mechanical assembly.
- end-of-line test.
- serialization and label application.
- carton packout.
- outgoing inspection.

Require quote breakdown for materials, labor, NRE/tooling, test fixture, packaging, freight assumption, scrap, warranty reserve, MOQ, lead time, payment terms, and component alternates.

CM must not substitute printer, PSU, battery, locks, paper, RF module, or content-storage components without written engineering change approval.
