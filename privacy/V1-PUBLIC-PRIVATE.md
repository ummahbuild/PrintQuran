# Public vs Private
Public allowed: complete Qur'ans printed, current Qur'an, current surah, ayah-output progress, ready/printing/service state.
Public prohibited: amount, total money, donor identity, donor count, donation count, recent donations, timestamps, averages, cash/card split.
Cash controller does not need denomination data. Reliability telemetry may include jams, cuts, paper, firmware/content versions, uptime, service events and completed print jobs.
