# Mechanical Specification

## Target envelope
Reference v0.2 envelope: **340 W × 300 D × 390 H mm**. Design goal is a low, wide countertop/floor-stand appliance rather than the taller concepts.

## Internal zoning
Top/front service bay:
- paper roll and printer.
- controller PCB/display.
- low-voltage power distribution.

Lower/rear secure zone:
- removable cash cassette.
- donation chute termination.

No service access panel may expose cash.

## Donation slot
Target clear opening: **135–155 mm wide × 8–11 mm high**, enough for folded notes without looking like a large open mouth. Gold bezel approximately 170–190 mm wide. Chute must include at least two direction changes or equivalent anti-fishing baffles.

## Paper
Design for 80 mm width and up to **83 mm OD** in base chassis if packaging permits. A 73–76 mm OD roll is preferred for common 70 m / heavyweight options.

## Serviceability
- paper roll replacement without tools after unlocking service lid.
- printer module replaceable with one screwdriver in <=10 minutes.
- PCB and display replaceable without drilling/rivets.
- harness connectors keyed and labeled.
- cash cassette removable independently.

## Mounting
Four hidden M6-equivalent anchor points in base. Provide optional wall/furniture tether kit.
