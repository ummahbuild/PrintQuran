# Non-Electronic Donation Modes
Cash Notes: narrow note slot, baffled chute, dual optical sensors, locked cassette. Detect event only, never value.

Manual Staff: momentary industrial button, spring-return key switch or foot pedal after staff accepts donation separately. Lowest mechanical complexity.

Envelope: separate top plate with roughly 170-190 mm wide x 12-18 mm high opening, anti-return geometry, deeper chute and dedicated cassette.

Coins: optional dedicated chute. Basic version detects passage only; currency-validating acceptors are regional and higher maintenance.

External Relay: opto-isolated dry-contact input from an existing donation system. One valid pulse creates one print credit.
