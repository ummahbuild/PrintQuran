# Physical Security Design

Threats: casual fishing, prying, opportunistic carry-away theft, unauthorized service-door opening, cash-door attack, insertion of foreign objects, and tamper attempts.

Controls:
- steel structural chassis.
- narrow slot with rounded bezel and anti-fishing chute.
- cash vault physically isolated.
- concealed hinge pins.
- recessed lock geometry.
- tamper switches on cash and service doors.
- internal anchor bolts.
- alarm event logging.
- no public external fasteners giving direct vault access.

Do not market as a safe or burglary-rated product unless independently tested/certified. DVT should include pry, fishing, tip, drop, and anchor tests with an agreed threat model.
