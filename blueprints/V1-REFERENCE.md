# V1 Reference Blueprint
Front: narrow donation slot at top, small status screen below, 80 mm paper outlet below screen; no public controls on PQ-CASH.

Side: slot -> >=70 mm drop -> baffle -> sensor A -> second direction change -> sensor B -> cash cassette. Printer/roll/controller remain behind a full steel service/cash divider.

Electrical: certified 12 V PSU -> protected 12 V printer rail + efficient buck rails -> display/controller -> sensors/printer/display/service USB.

Printer bay starting clearance: 150 W x 125 H x 120 D, replaced by selected supplier STEP. Roll bay >=83 mm OD x 80 mm. Paper outlet ~92 W x 14 H, downward 10-20 degrees. Service opening target >=230 W x 160 H.
