# Packaging Specification
Goals: survive parcel freight, protect printer/cutter and display, minimize plastic, prevent cosmetic abrasion.

Packout:
- device in nonwoven/PE sleeve as qualified.
- molded pulp or EPE corner/end caps.
- accessory box: PSU, keys in tamper-evident envelope, anchor kit, quick-start, starter paper roll if supplied.
- cash cassette secured for transport.
- printer transport restraint if supplier requires.

DVT packaging tests: drop sequence, vibration, compression/stacking, temperature/humidity conditioning as appropriate to distribution route.
