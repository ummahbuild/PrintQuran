# Telemetry Policy
Allowed operational telemetry when enabled:
- device online/offline.
- printer error class.
- paper-low estimate.
- cutter/head usage.
- firmware/content versions.
- battery health.
- service/cash door tamper event.

Do not publish donation amounts, donor count, donation timestamps, or payment history to the public display. Fleet backend financial telemetry is out of the core device scope and must follow deployment privacy/accounting rules.
