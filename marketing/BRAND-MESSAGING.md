# Brand and Messaging
Working primary identity: Print Qur'an / PrintQuran. 'Sadaqah to Qur'an' can function as campaign/category language. Avoid using 'Zakat' as the general product identity unless a deployment is explicitly structured for eligible zakat collection.

Visual system: Midnight Black, Deep Brown and Islamic Blue over one common chassis; restrained gold bezel/pattern accents. Qur'an strip remains visually separate from commercial branding.

Audience messages: Mosques - private giving plus immediate reading. Donors - whatever you give, the next surah prints. Manufacturers - commodity internal architecture, secure steel cash path, deterministic verified content and field-replaceable modules.
