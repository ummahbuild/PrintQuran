# Industrial Design Brief
Form: low and wide, stable, dignified; avoid tall vending-machine proportions. Target 340 × 300 × 390 mm reference envelope.

Visual hierarchy:
1. narrow gold cash slot/bezel.
2. optional gold amount dial and NFC reader directly adjacent.
3. privacy-safe progress display.
4. paper exit.
5. subtle Qur’anic geometric pattern on side panels.

Do not print decorative pseudo-Arabic. Any Arabic shown in production UI must come from reviewed copy/content assets.
