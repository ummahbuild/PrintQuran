# Brand Architecture
The hardware platform should support white-label exterior panels and screen themes without changing core firmware integrity.

Candidate public brands:
- **Print Qur’an** — clearest functional/infrastructure name.
- **Sadaqah to Qur’an** — emotionally direct for voluntary giving deployments.
- **Sadaqah2Quran** — digital/startup variant.
- **Project 114** — campaign/institutional identity.

Avoid `Zakat2Quran` as the default unless a deployment has specifically established the relevant zakāt eligibility and governance; zakāt is not interchangeable with generic ṣadaqah.

Core palette options:
- Midnight Black + gold accents.
- Deep Brown + gold accents.
- Islamic Blue + gold accents.

Use one structural chassis. Color comes from powder coat/cosmetic side panels and printed UI theme.
