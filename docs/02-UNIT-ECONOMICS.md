# Unit Economics Targets

These are engineering targets, not supplier quotes.

## Target landed hardware cost, excluding payment terminal
- EVT 10 units: $250–$450 each due to prototype fabrication and retail modules.
- 100 units: $135–$210.
- 500 units: $110–$170.
- 1,000 units: $95–$145.
- 5,000 units: $80–$125.

## Cost-down priorities
1. Printer module negotiated direct/OEM.
2. Folded steel enclosure DFM and common chassis across colors.
3. Custom controller PCB after EVT rather than dev boards.
4. Standard external PSU rather than internal mains supply.
5. One display size and one harness architecture.
6. Cosmetic shell/pattern panels separated from structural chassis.
7. Regional payment reader treated as add-on.
8. Phenol-free paper sourced locally by region when qualified.

## Retail architecture
Base cash model and connected payment model should be separate SKUs. Do not force every customer to subsidize payment certification, cellular service, or an oversized battery.

## Consumables
Paper is the main recurring consumable. Do not make paper proprietary; qualify dimensions and chemistry, then publish an approved-vendor list.
