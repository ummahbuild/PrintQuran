# Field Service Manual

## Volunteer tasks
- replace paper.
- wipe exterior.
- read status screen.
- report error code.

## Authorized service tasks
- clear cutter jam.
- replace printer module.
- replace PSU/controller/display/sensor.
- battery replacement.
- firmware/content update.

## Cash collection
Cash key and service key must be operationally separable. Collection procedure must not require opening printer/service electronics.

## Preventive maintenance
Monthly: visual paper path/cutter dust check, locks, anchor, door sensors.
Quarterly: print quality test, chute inspection, cooling vents, harness inspection where accessible.
Annually: printer usage review, battery health where fitted, security/anchor check, firmware/content status.

## Cleaning
Use only printer-manufacturer-approved print-head cleaning procedure/material. Never spray liquid into the device. Power down before internal service.
