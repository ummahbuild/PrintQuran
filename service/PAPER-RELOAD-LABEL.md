# Inside-door label copy
1. UNLOCK SERVICE LID
2. LIFT PRINTER COVER
3. DROP IN 80 mm ROLL — COATED SIDE AS MARKED
4. PULL 10 cm OF PAPER FORWARD
5. CLOSE PRINTER UNTIL IT CLICKS
6. CLOSE + LOCK SERVICE LID
7. DEVICE FEEDS/CUTS TEST PAPER AUTOMATICALLY

If RED status remains: scan service QR or report the error code. Never open the cash door to replace paper.
