# Supplier Qualification

## Critical components
Printer, PSU, paper, cash lock, battery/BMS, PCB assembly, steel chassis, and payment terminal are critical.

## Printer supplier scorecard
Require:
- exact printable width and dpi.
- 12 V power/current waveform, not just nominal current.
- head rated distance and cutter rated cycles.
- paper thickness/grammage range.
- cutter jam recovery behavior.
- status/error interface documentation.
- UART/USB/RS-232 protocol documentation.
- Linux/MCU driver availability or ESC/POS command support.
- sample lead time, MOQ, production lead time, warranty.
- PCN/EOL notice policy.
- factory quality certification.

Buy 3–5 samples from at least three candidates and run the same 10k-cycle fixture test.

## Paper qualification
Approve by paper grade, not only reseller SKU. Record width, OD, core, gsm, coating side, phenol-free declaration, image-life claim, curl after printing, dust, cutter behavior, black density, storage conditions, and supplier lot traceability.

Current public examples show 80 mm phenol-free/BPA-free/BPS-free supply in multiple lengths and grammages; the project should still perform independent incoming verification and supplier document review.
