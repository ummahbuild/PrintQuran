# RFQ — Sheet Metal Enclosure

Material target: cold-rolled steel, 1.2 mm structural shell; 1.5 mm cash vault where required. Powder coat exterior, zinc/passivation or equivalent corrosion control on hidden surfaces where appropriate.

Quote prototype laser-cut/bent and production tooling options at 10/100/500/1k/5k. Include welding, PEM hardware, powder coat, inspection, packaging, and dimensional capability.

Critical features:
- cash/service compartment separation.
- concealed or internal hinges.
- anchor points.
- replaceable cosmetic side panels.
- printer alignment datum.
- no sharp internal service edges.
