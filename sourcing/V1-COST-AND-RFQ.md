# V1 Cost Targets and RFQ
Engineering targets at about 1,000 units: printer $28-45; controller PCBA $10-16; small display $4-8; steel chassis $18-28; cash cassette/chute $10-18; locks $5-9; sensors/doors $2-4; certified PSU $7-11; harness/fasteners $5-8; bezel/cosmetics $4-7; assembly/EOL $7-12; packaging $5-8. Indicative target roughly $100-174 before freight, duty, certification, warranty reserve, margin or optional payment terminal. These are targets, not supplier quotes.

RFQs must quote 10/100/500/1000/5000 units and separate metalwork, coating, PCBA, harness, printer, display, locks/cassette, PSU, assembly, EOL, packaging, NRE/tooling and certification support. Return MOQ, lead time, origin, alternates, PCN/EOL policy, warranty, capacity and DFM recommendations.
