# Incoming Inspection

Printer lot: sample print density, cutter, firmware revision, connector, mounting dimensions.
Paper lot: width/OD/core/gsm declaration, phenol-free CoC where required, curl, density, dust visual check.
PSU: model/certification markings/plug, no unapproved substitution.
Steel: coating, thickness sample, critical dimensions, burrs.
Locks: keying and retention.
PCBAs: AOI report, programming identity, sample functional test.
Batteries: supplier lot, voltage, BMS, shipping documents.
