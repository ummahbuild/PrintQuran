# Compliance Plan — Engineering Planning Only

Exact obligations depend on sale country and payment module.

Likely workstreams:
- electrical/product safety for AV/ICT equipment and external PSU evidence (IEC/UL 62368-1 family commonly relevant).
- EMC emissions/immunity.
- radio approvals for Wi-Fi/BLE module/device by market.
- RoHS/REACH/material declarations where applicable.
- battery transport/safety documentation for optional LiFePO4 SKU.
- WEEE/e-waste and packaging obligations where applicable.
- payment terminal PCI/EMV/provider certifications handled by selected certified payment system, not recreated in the core device.
- accessibility/local language labeling requirements as applicable.

Before certification, commission a qualified compliance lab to write the formal test plan for target markets. Do not rely on this repository as legal/compliance advice.
