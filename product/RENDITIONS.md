# Renditions
- PQ-CASH: cash-only offline base; no amount dial.
- PQ-MANUAL: staff button/key/foot pedal after donation is accepted separately.
- PQ-ENVELOPE: dedicated envelope chute/cassette.
- PQ-TAP: certified external payment terminal; no cash vault required.
- PQ-HYBRID: cash + certified terminal; amount selector only affects electronic payments.
- PQ-PRINT: isolated external trigger or sponsored button.
- PQ-EDU: donation-free educational sequential printing.
All share the same verified-content, print, sequence and service architecture.
